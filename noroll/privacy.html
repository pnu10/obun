Privacy Policy

NoRoll · Last updated 17 November 2026

Short version: NoRoll does not collect anything. There is no account, no sign-in and no server. Your photos stay in the app on your device, and we never see them.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no server and no database, and the app makes no network connections of its own. NoRoll has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind, and it does not ask for your name, email or any other identifier.

Your photos and videos

Photos and videos you take, import or share into NoRoll are stored in NoRoll's own private storage on your device, organized in the folders you create. They are not added to your camera roll or iCloud Photos, and they are not sent anywhere. They leave the app only when you share, save or export them yourself.

Like any app's data, NoRoll's storage can be included in an iPhone backup you make yourself (iCloud Backup or a computer backup). That backup belongs to your Apple account; we have no access to it.

Text in photos

To let you search photos by the words in them, NoRoll reads text in your photos using Apple's on-device text recognition. This happens entirely on your iPhone. The recognized text is saved next to the photo in NoRoll's storage and is never uploaded.

Permissions, and why

  • Camera — to take photos and videos inside NoRoll. Used only while the camera screen is open.
  • Microphone — to record sound when you shoot a video.
  • Photo library (read) — only when you import photos, and, if you turn on "Remove from Photos After Import", to remove those originals from your library. iOS asks you to confirm every deletion.
  • Photo library (add) — only when you choose to save photos out of NoRoll.
  • Face ID — to lock the app or individual folders. NoRoll only receives a yes/no result from iOS; it never sees your face data.

You can refuse or withdraw any of these in iOS Settings. The rest of the app keeps working.

Purchases

NoRoll is sold through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple, and we receive no payment details — not your card, your name or your address.

Children

NoRoll collects no data from anyone, children included.

Deleting your data

Deleted photos move to Recently Deleted inside the app and are removed permanently after 30 days, or right away if you delete them there. Deleting the app removes everything at once. Because nothing is stored outside your device, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Changes

If a future version of NoRoll ever handles data differently, we will update this page and change the date at the top before that version ships.

사진과 동영상

NoRoll로 찍거나 가져오거나 공유해 넣은 사진·동영상은 직접 만든 폴더에 담겨 기기 안 NoRoll 전용 저장소에만 저장됩니다. 사진첩이나 iCloud 사진에 추가되지 않고 어디로도 전송되지 않습니다. 직접 공유·저장·내보내기를 할 때만 앱 밖으로 나갑니다.

다른 앱과 마찬가지로, 직접 만드는 iPhone 백업(iCloud 백업 또는 컴퓨터 백업)에는 NoRoll 데이터가 포함될 수 있습니다. 이 백업은 사용자의 Apple 계정에 속하며 저희는 접근할 수 없습니다.

사진 속 글자

사진 속 글자로 검색할 수 있도록 Apple의 기기 내 문자 인식으로 사진의 글자를 읽습니다. 이 과정은 모두 iPhone 안에서 이루어지며, 인식한 글자는 사진과 함께 NoRoll 저장소에만 저장되고 업로드되지 않습니다.

권한과 사용 목적

  • 카메라 — NoRoll 안에서 사진·동영상을 찍기 위해. 촬영 화면이 열려 있을 때만 씁니다.
  • 마이크 — 동영상을 찍을 때 소리를 녹음하기 위해.
  • 사진 보관함(읽기) — 사진을 가져올 때, 그리고 '가져온 뒤 사진첩에서 삭제'를 켠 경우 원본을 사진첩에서 지우기 위해. 삭제할 때마다 iOS가 확인을 받습니다.
  • 사진 보관함(추가) — 사진을 사진첩에 저장하기로 할 때만.
  • Face ID — 앱이나 폴더를 잠그기 위해. NoRoll은 iOS로부터 성공/실패 결과만 받으며 얼굴 정보는 볼 수 없습니다.

iOS 설정에서 언제든 거부하거나 철회할 수 있으며, 나머지 기능은 그대로 쓸 수 있습니다.
